Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026 • Savoiré AI v2.0 by Sooban Talha Technologies • Founder: Sooban Talha

🔒 Your data is safe • No selling • Encrypted • Firebase + Razorpay Secure

1. Introduction

Welcome to Savoiré AI (savoireai.vercel.app), a product of Sooban Talha Technologies. We build AI-powered study tools - Notes, Flashcards, Quiz, Summary, Mind Maps. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By using Savoiré AI, you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the service.

2. What We Collect

A) Account Info (via Firebase Auth)

  • Email, Display Name, Profile Photo (if you use Google Login)
  • Firebase UID (anonymous identifier)
  • Login timestamps

B) Study Data (via Firestore)

  • Topics you enter, Generated study content (notes, flashcards, quiz, etc.) - stored in users/{uid}/history and saved
  • Credit balance: free credits (10,000 welcome), purchased credits, used credits, plan
  • Streak, sessions, total words, best streak
  • Usage history: tool used, credits consumed, timestamps

C) Payment Data (via Razorpay)

  • We DO NOT store your card number, UPI PIN, CVV, or bank password. Razorpay handles all payment securely (PCI DSS Level 1, RBI authorized).
  • We store: Payment ID, Order ID, Amount, Plan, Credits added, Status - in transactions collection and optional Google Sheet for your records
  • Razorpay may collect phone number for UPI verification - handled by Razorpay, not us

D) Technical Data

  • Device type, browser, IP (for rate limiting and abuse prevention)
  • AI generation logs: topic, tool, duration, tokens used (for debugging and Google Sheets analytics if you enabled GOOGLE_WEBHOOK_URL)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI study generation (Mesh API - amazon/nova-micro)
  • To show your credit balance, history, saved notes across devices
  • To process payments and add credits via Razorpay
  • To send you welcome email via Resend/Gmail ([email protected]) - only if you enabled RESEND_API_KEY
  • To improve our AI prompts and reliability (anonymized usage stats)
  • To prevent abuse (rate limiting, token exhaustion check)

4. Where We Store Data

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: Google Cloud, location asia-south1 (Mumbai) - Encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Vercel: Serverless functions hosting (Washington DC) - Logs for debugging, env vars encrypted
  • Razorpay: Payment data - PCI DSS Level 1, RBI authorized Payment Aggregator, settlement to your bank via NEFT/IMPS
  • Mesh API: AI generation - Topic sent to generate notes, not stored long-term by Mesh
  • Google Sheets (optional): If you set GOOGLE_WEBHOOK_URL, we log anonymized usage (user, topic, tool, duration) for your analytics

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Auth + Firestore - Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay: Payments (UPI, Cards, NetBanking) - Privacy Policy - PCI DSS Level 1
  • Mesh API: AI models - Privacy
  • Vercel: Hosting - Privacy
  • Resend / Gmail (optional): Welcome emails - only if you configure

6. What We DON'T Do

  • We DO NOT sell your data to advertisers
  • We DO NOT store your UPI PIN, Card CVV, or bank password - ever
  • We DO NOT read your private study content for ads - only for generation
  • We DO NOT share your email with third parties except as needed for service (Firebase, Razorpay)

7. Your Rights

  • Access: See all your data in Dashboard -> Settings -> Export Data (JSON backup)
  • Delete: Dashboard -> Settings -> Delete Account (3-step confirmation) - Deletes Firestore data + Firebase Auth user + all history/saved. Takes 1-2 minutes to prevent exploit.
  • Logout: Avatar dropdown -> Log Out clears localStorage and signs out
  • Correction: Change display name/photo in Settings or via Google account
  • Data Portability: Export JSON from Settings

8. Google OAuth Email - Why You Get "You shared data..." Email?

When you login with Google, Google sends you a security email "You granted access to Savoiré AI to see your name and photo" - This is from Google, not from us, for your safety. It cannot be disabled as per Google policy. It shows our app name Savoiré AI and logo. You also get our custom welcome email from [email protected] with 10,000 free credits info.

9. Cookies & Storage

  • We use localStorage for: Firebase ID token (sv_firebase_token), user name, avatar, streak, sessions, theme prefs - to work offline and fast
  • We use sessionStorage for Firebase Auth redirect handling (for mobile login)
  • We use Firestore for cloud sync across devices (history, saved, credits, streak)
  • No third-party tracking cookies, no ads

10. Children's Privacy

Savoiré AI is for students 13+. If you are under 13, use with parent/guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will show new date at top. Continued use after update means acceptance.
