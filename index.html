Transparency · What we ask for, and why

Only what we need. Nothing more.

When you sign in, we request the minimum data to personalise your workspace and keep your study material safe across devices.

👤

Your name

To greet you on the dashboard and label your profile. You can choose a custom display name after sign-in — Google's stored name is only used if you opt in.

📧

Your email

To create your account, sync your credits, history and saved notes across devices, and send a one-time welcome email from noreply@savoireai.vercel.app. Your email is never sold or shared.

🖼️

Profile photo (optional)

To show your avatar in the dashboard header. You can pick an emoji avatar instead — your photo is only kept if you actively choose it.

🔒
What we never do: we don't store your Google password, we don't read your Gmail, Drive or Calendar, we don't sell your data, we don't run ads. Your study topics are processed to generate your material and stored under strict per-user rules (allow read, write if auth.uid == uid) in Firestore (Mumbai region).

Our AI engine

Powered by Mesh API — fourteen models, one orchestration layer.

Savoiré AI doesn't rely on a single model. We route every generation through Mesh API, which races 14+ models in parallel using first-success racing. The fastest valid response wins, and credits are only deducted on success. This is how we deliver consistent quality, low latency and predictable cost across every topic.

About Savoiré AI

Savoiré AI is a premium AI study companion built for students everywhere. 10,000 welcome credits on signup — top-up packs from Rs 19. It turns any topic into notes, flashcards, quizzes, summaries and mind maps in 20+ languages.

Version 2.0 was designed around one idea: studying should feel like a conversation with a brilliant tutor, not a fight with a blank page. Every tool streams its output live, so you watch your material take shape instead of waiting on it.
