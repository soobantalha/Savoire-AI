How credits are charged

  • Credits are calculated as (input characters + output characters) ÷ 4 multiplied by a per-tool factor (Notes 1.0×, Summary 0.6×, Flashcards 1.2×, Quiz 1.1×, Mind Map 1.3×, Mega Bundle 2.8×).

  • All pack credits are valid for 30 days from the date of purchase. We may extend validity for promotional packs at our discretion.

Payments

  • All payments are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorised Payment Aggregator with PCI DSS Level 1 certification.
  • Supported instruments: UPI, Credit & Debit Cards, NetBanking, Wallets. Razorpay deducts its standard fee (≈2% + GST) before settlement to our bank via NEFT / IMPS in T+2 / T+3 working days.
  • We never see or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank password.

Refunds

  • Credits that have been used to generate content are non-refundable. Once consumed, the output is yours but the credits are spent.
  • Unused credits on a freshly purchased pack can be refunded within 7 days of purchase, minus Razorpay processing fees, by writing to our support email. We process eligible refunds within 7 working days.
